ALF-238 — Add "Send to Instapaper" button in Reader

Refinement spec Story in epic ALF-232 · Reader Module. Both of the epic's stories are built and merged (read ALF-232-notes.md for where the build moved from the epic). Written 2026-09-24.

In short. The Reader row's primary verb stops being Open and becomes Send to Instapaper. One press saves the post to the owner's Instapaper account and archives it in the Reader. It uses Instapaper's Full API, and the request carries the post's own email HTML, so a paid post the owner subscribes to arrives in full rather than as the web paywall's teaser. The original stays reachable through a quiet Original ↗ link. The Instapaper call runs server-side in a Next.js route handler. Its four OAuth credentials live in Vercel env, and nothing Instapaper-related ever reaches the browser.

This is one story across three layers: a migration, a small Worker change so intake keeps the email HTML, and the frontend (Instapaper client, route, store action, row).

1 · Context & problem

The Reader's two verbs came from Steve Newman's reader. Open goes to the original, and Archive dismisses the post (ALF-232 D11). The owner doesn't read sources where they live. They collect them in Instapaper and read there. So the useful verb for a post worth reading is "put this in my Instapaper", not "open it in a browser tab". Opening a tab today means copying the link into Instapaper by hand.

The owner also noted it "will play nicer with the eventual wiki". The LLM-wiki epic (ALF-259) already plans an Instapaper tag-sync producer as a follow-up. The owner tags an article in Instapaper, and its body and highlights file themselves into the wiki's inbox. A post sent from the Reader joins that same pipe, so Instapaper becomes the single place reading happens and the wiki's one source for highlights.

2 · Decisions

The owner settled D1–D5 in this refinement session. D6–D10 follow from them and from the code as it stands.

D1Instapaper's Full API, signed with OAuth 1.0a owner

POST https://www.instapaper.com/api/1/bookmarks/add, signed HMAC-SHA1 with four server-only values: INSTAPAPER_CONSUMER_KEY, INSTAPAPER_CONSUMER_SECRET, INSTAPAPER_ACCESS_TOKEN, INSTAPAPER_ACCESS_TOKEN_SECRET. A fifth, optional INSTAPAPER_API_URL (default https://www.instapaper.com) exists so the E2E mock can stand in. No password is ever stored. Instapaper's terms allow one only for the one-time xAuth exchange, which happens outside the app (§10).

Why not the Simple API: it takes a username and password and only a URL. Instapaper then fetches the page itself, so a paid post saves as its public teaser, a post with no web link can't be sent at all, and no bookmark id comes back. An app registered in Instapaper's Owner Only mode works for its owner's account with no review, which is all a single-user system needs.

D2A send carries the post's body, and that body is the email HTML, kept at intake owner

A new nullable column, reader_posts.html, holds the decoded text/html part that the Worker already has in hand. The route sends it as Instapaper's content parameter, and Instapaper parses the article out of it, the same way its own email-in feature handles a forwarded newsletter. The body falls back down a ladder:

  1. html when present;
  2. else text rendered as HTML paragraphs, for posts ingested before this ships;
  3. else no body. With a link, Instapaper fetches the URL itself. Without one, nothing is sent (D7).

The 90-day sweep nulls html in the same statement that nulls text.

Why the HTML rather than the stored text: the stored text loses images, headings, lists and links. The owner reads in Instapaper, so the body should read like the post. Why store it rather than re-fetch: Gmail credentials live only in the Worker, and a send is a browser-initiated act on Vercel. Storage is small at the measured volume: at a guessed 5–10× HTML-to-text ratio, it's roughly 65–130 KB a post × ~4 a day × 90 days ≈ 25–50 MB before Postgres' TOAST compression, which shrinks inline-styled markup several-fold. That is set against a 21 MB database.

D3Sending archives the post owner

One press does both. On the reading list the row plays the same exit collapse as Archive and leaves. The post is then in the archive carrying an in Instapaper badge. Sending from the archive view doesn't move the row, and the badge appears in place. A sent post that is later unarchived keeps its badge.

Why: once a post is in Instapaper, that's where it lives. Leaving it on the list would make the owner dismiss every sent post twice.

D4Open becomes a quiet "Original ↗" link owner

The outline Open button is removed. The same target (postOpenLink, unchanged: canonical URL → Gmail permalink → nothing) is reached through a ghost, muted-text Original ↗ link. It sits in the overview panel's footer, next to Re-summarise, whenever the row has a panel. A row with no panel (a first-time pending post, or a failed or refused one) carries the link at the end of its verb row instead. Every row with a link therefore has exactly one way out. Clicking it still stamps opened_at.

Why two placements: today a row only gets a panel when it has an overview, a stamp or a re-run verb (hasPanel). Adding a panel just to hold a link would put an "Overview" toggle on rows that have no overview, and the link can't sit on the gist line because that line is inside the card's <button>. D12's rule that a post is never left without a way out still holds: when Send fails or can't run, Original is the way out.

D5No tag and no folder; the gist is the bookmark's description owner

Sent posts land in Instapaper's Unread list like anything else the owner saves. The request passes title (the post's title, so Instapaper skips its synchronous title lookup) and, when a summary exists, description = the gist, so the Instapaper list shows alfred's one-paragraph take under each post. No tags, no folder_id.

Why: ALF-259's tag-sync keys on a tag the owner applies by hand as an act of choosing. An automatic tag would either do nothing or send everything to the wiki.

D6The call runs in a Next.js route handler, never in the browser and never in the Worker

POST /api/reader/posts/[id]/instapaper, session-gated through withSession, on the Node runtime (node:crypto for HMAC-SHA1 and the nonce). The route reads the body server-side, signs, sends, and on success stamps the row. It is the only reader of html/text outside the Worker.

Why: the credentials can't reach the browser, and a send is a synchronous act the owner is waiting on. The Worker is cron-driven and has no endpoint the browser calls. The PR-ratio route is the house pattern for exactly this shape.

D7A post with no web link goes as a private bookmark

When canonical_url isn't an http(s) URL but a body exists, the request sets is_private_from_source=email and omits url. That is Instapaper's own mechanism for mail with no permanent URL. The Gmail permalink is never sent as a URL, because it would save a login wall. When there's neither a link nor a body, the verb is disabled (D8). All 6 of today's link-less posts have text.

D8When the verb can't work, it is disabled and says why

The Send button renders disabled (disabled:opacity-50) with a title in two cases:

  • This deployment has no Instapaper credentials. The Work instance and local dev are both in this case. Title: "Instapaper isn't set up on this deployment." The shell layout passes instapaperConfigured = getInstapaperConfig() !== null into ReaderProvider.
  • The post has nothing to send: no http(s) link, and either text_swept_at is set or word_count === 0. Title: "No link and no stored text to send."

Why not fall back to the old Open button when unconfigured: the old button would be a second row shape to keep alive for deployments that don't use the Reader. Original ↗ still works everywhere.

D9Keyboard: i sends, o still opens the original

ReaderHotkeyAction gains 'send' on i, which runs the same function as the button and does nothing when the button is disabled. o keeps 'open' and opens the Original target even when that link sits inside a collapsed panel. That panel is inert (AnimatedHeightCollapse), so the key can't just click the anchor the way it does today. Instead the key and the link share one openOriginal(): window.open(href, '_blank', 'noopener,noreferrer') for the key, the anchor's native navigation for a click, and markOpened for both. Hints (desktop, selected row only, as today) show i beside Send and o beside Original when it's visible.

Why i: in Comms, i already means "send this somewhere else" (to the Inbox). Moving o to Send would break the one mnemonic the list already teaches.

D10Failures roll back and give the reason; there's no retry queue

The store action is optimistic (runOptimisticMutation), like Archive. If the send fails, the row comes back and a toast states the route's reason (§7), such as "This publication has opted out of Instapaper". Nothing retries on its own, and nothing is written to the row unless Instapaper confirms the save. A second press while a send is in flight is ignored.

Why: the owner is watching. A send that quietly retries later would be a verb whose outcome they can't see.

6 · The Instapaper client

Source: Instapaper's developer docs (instapaper.com/developers/v1/full-api/…) as of 2026-09-24. The pages are rendered client-side, so a fetcher sees only a title. The markdown behind them is served as /assets/<page>-<hash>.md, and the hashed names are listed in the site's /js/appCollectionManagement-*.js bundle. The pages used here are bookmark-api, authentication, responses and api-terms.

A new server-only folder, frontend/lib/instapaper/, with each file co-located with its test:

FileExportsBehaviour
config.tsgetInstapaperConfig() Reads the four credential vars and INSTAPAPER_API_URL by literal name, as in lib/github/config.ts. Returns null unless all four credentials are non-empty. import 'server-only'.
oauth.tssignRequest(method, url, params, creds, { nonce, timestamp }) → Authorization header OAuth 1.0a HMAC-SHA1 per RFC 5849: percent-encode per §3.6, and build the base string from the method, the base URL, and the sorted oauth_* plus form-body parameters (§3.4.1.3). The key is consumerSecret&tokenSecret. nonce/timestamp are injectable so the signature is deterministic under test.
content.tstextToHtml(text) HTML-escapes the text, then wraps each non-empty line in <p> (the stored text keeps one line per block).
bookmark.tsbuildBookmarkParams(post), addBookmark(config, params) Builds the form params (table below) and POSTs them application/x-www-form-urlencoded; charset=utf-8 with a 15 s abort. The result is a discriminated outcome: { kind: 'saved', bookmarkId } | { kind: 'refused', code } | { kind: 'unavailable' }.

Request parameters

ParamValueWhen
urlcanonical_urlWhen it parses as http:/https:, the same isWebUrl rule open-link.ts uses (share it; don't copy it).
is_private_from_sourceemailNo url and a body exists (D7).

If there's no url and no body, buildBookmarkParams returns null and the route answers 409 without calling Instapaper.

Reading Instapaper's answer

Instapaper answers with a JSON array. A success holds a { "type": "bookmark", "bookmark_id": … } object, and a failure holds { "type": "error", "error_code": …, "message": … }. Its message is "not intended to be displayed to users", so it's logged and never shown. Instapaper's docs say to treat a body that isn't JSON as a 503.

7 · The route

frontend/app/api/reader/posts/[id]/instapaper/route.ts — POST, no body, withSession.

  1. parseUUID; then getInstapaperConfig(), which answers 501 when unset before any read.
  2. Read the post with a new data function in lib/data/reader.ts, getReaderPostForSend(supabase, id), which selects title, canonical_url, gist, html, text, archived_at. It is the only frontend read of html/text, and its result never leaves the route. Missing → 404.
  3. buildBookmarkParams → null → 409.
  4. addBookmark, then map the outcome (table). If there's no bookmark, nothing is written.
  5. On saved, run a new markReaderPostSent(supabase, id, bookmarkId, now, alreadyArchivedAt), which writes instapaper_sent_at = now, instapaper_bookmark_id and archived_at = alreadyArchivedAt ?? now, selecting READER_POST_LIST_COLUMNS. Return 200 with the row.

8 · Store, row & keyboard

Store — lib/stores/reader-store.tsx

  • ReaderProvider takes instapaperConfigured: boolean. The shell layout passes it, and renderWithProviders' reader seed and the Reader stories' decorators gain the option, defaulting to true. The row reads it through a hook such as useInstapaperConfigured().
  • ReaderActions.sendToInstapaper(id): Promise<ReaderPostListItem>. It follows setArchived's shape exactly: capture only the touched fields, beginWrite/endWrite, and runOptimisticMutation with an optimistic patch of { instapaper_sent_at: now, archived_at: current.archived_at ?? now }. It reconciles by replace and rolls back the captured fields. onError toasts the route's detail for 409 / 422 / 429 / 501 / 502, and "Couldn't send that post to Instapaper" otherwise, following the pattern of today's refusal().
  • The replaceAll keep-rule needs nothing new, because a sent post is archived, and archived rows already survive an active read.

Row — components/reader/post-row.tsx

  • Verb row order: Send to Instapaper (outline, sm, hint i) · Overview/Hide overview (ghost) · Retry summary (outline, failed/refused only) · Archive/Unarchive (outline) · Original ↗ (ghost, muted, only when the row has no panel).

  • Marker: PostMarkers takes the post (or an extra prop) and adds a secondary badge in Instapaper whenever instapaper_sent_at is set, beside any summary-state badge.
  • Copy: the pending placeholder becomes "…send it now, or check back in a few minutes." The failed/refused tail becomes "The post is still here; send it or archive it." The swept tail becomes "…so it can't be retried; send it or archive it."
  • lib/data/reader.ts's module comment ("the 'Open' verb sends the owner to the original") is updated to match.

10 · Credentials & setup

The owner has a "send to Instapaper" skill in their Claude account that holds a token. A Full API token is bound to the consumer key that issued it, so it can be reused only together with that key and its secret.

  1. If the skill holds all four values (consumer key/secret, token/secret), reuse them as they are.
  2. Otherwise: register an application at instapaper.com/developers/applications/create. It starts in Owner Only mode, which is enough and needs no review. Then run the one-off exchange, npm run instapaper:token -w frontend. It prompts for the consumer key/secret and the Instapaper username/password, POSTs a signed /api/1/oauth/access_token with x_auth_mode=client_auth, and prints oauth_token/oauth_token_secret. It reuses lib/instapaper/oauth.ts rather than keeping a second signer, and it never writes the password anywhere (Instapaper's API terms).
  3. Set the four vars on the Personal instance's Vercel project (Production), then redeploy. The Work instance stays unset and gets D8's disabled verb.
  4. Document the four vars and INSTAPAPER_API_URL in frontend/.env.example in the PR-ratio block's style: server-side only, never NEXT_PUBLIC_, and what "unset" means.

12 · Acceptance criteria

  • Instapaper client
  • getInstapaperConfig() is null unless all four credential vars are set. None of them is NEXT_PUBLIC_, and nothing under lib/instapaper/ is importable from a client component (server-only).
  • The signer reproduces a published OAuth 1.0a HMAC-SHA1 test vector and includes form-body parameters in the base string.
  • The request follows §6's parameter table: content ladder html ▸ text-as-paragraphs ▸ none, a private bookmark when there's no http(s) link, the gist as description, and no tags or folder.
  • Every row of §7's outcome table maps as written. Instapaper's own message is never shown to the owner.
  • Route
  • POST /api/reader/posts/[id]/instapaper is session-gated. It answers 501 with no read and no outbound call when unconfigured, 404 for an unknown post, and 409 when there's nothing to send.
  • On success it stamps instapaper_sent_at, instapaper_bookmark_id, and archived_at (keeping an existing value), and returns the row with neither text nor html.
  • On any Instapaper failure the row is not written.
  • No log line contains credentials, the Authorization header, or post content.
  • Data & Worker
  • The migration (next free number) adds html, instapaper_sent_at, and instapaper_bookmark_id, and reader_sweep_text nulls html with text. The DB assertion proves it.
  • Types are regenerated. ReaderPostListItem omits text and html. READER_POST_LIST_COLUMNS carries the two Instapaper columns and not html.
  • extractPost returns the raw HTML for an HTML-extracted post within READER_HTML_CHARS, and none otherwise. The intake insert stores it, and the tick's subrequest count is unchanged.
  • Row, store & keyboard (the mockups)
  • Open is gone. Send to Instapaper leads the verb row in every state A–F.
  • Original ↗ sits in the panel footer when the row has a panel (B), and at the end of the verb row otherwise (C–E). It points at postOpenLink's target, stamps opened_at, and is absent when there's no link.
  • Sending from the list collapses the row out, and it appears in the archive with in Instapaper (F). Sending from the archive adds the badge in place.
  • A failed send restores the row and toasts the route's sentence (G). A second press while a send is in flight does nothing.
  • Send is disabled, with the matching title, on an unconfigured deployment and on a post with no link and no stored text (E).
  • The placeholder copy says "send", not "open" (C, D, and the swept tail).
  • i sends the selected row and o opens its original, including a done row whose panel is closed. Neither does anything when its target is unavailable. Hints show i on Send and o on a visible Original, on desktop only.
  • At 375 px the three verbs fit on one line with no horizontal scroll (phone mockup).
  • Tests, docs, demo
  • §11's suites exist and are green, the moved Storybook baselines are approved, and the E2E covers a send and a refusal against the mock.
  • .env.example documents the five vars. database/README.md lists the new columns. npm run instapaper:token -w frontend exists and uses the shared signer.
  • A demo doc under docs/demos/ follows the showboat skill. The PR lists the owner's after-deploy checks (§11).

13 · Out of scope & open questions

Out of scope

  • Instapaper → wiki. Tag-sync, pulling highlights, and the bookmark-id identity it will key on are ALF-259's follow-up. This story only stores the id so that producer has something to match.
  • Batch send ("send every unread post") and auto-send. Instapaper's terms forbid adding bookmarks without a per-item user action anyway.
  • Reading Instapaper back: read progress, "read in Instapaper" status, un-sending.
  • A backfill of html for posts ingested before this ships. They take the text rung.
  • Cleaning the email HTML before sending (stripping Substack's chrome). It depends on open question 1.
  • Comms. Nothing in Comms changes.

Open questions

  1. Does Instapaper's parser pick the article out of Substack's email HTML cleanly? Its email-in feature suggests yes, and the after-deploy check answers it. If the "READ IN APP" buttons or the footer dominate, a follow-up can send only the post's body container. Nothing in this story blocks on it.
  2. Should html be swept sooner than text? Sends happen within days of arrival, so a 30-day html window would cut its storage by about two-thirds. At the measured volume (§2 D2) it isn't worth a second sweep parameter. Revisit if reader_posts passes ~100 MB.
