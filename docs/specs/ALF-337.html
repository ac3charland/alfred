ALF-337 — Add an Inbox item from a Reader post

Every Reader post gets an “Add to Inbox” verb that opens a one-line field under the row; whatever you type lands in the Inbox as a capture linked back to the post.

Revised 2026-10-13: D3 → B, a toast linking to the Inbox (your review); Plate II redrawn.

Decisions

  1. D1Where does the field live?

    A verb in the row’s verb line toggles a field directly beneath it

    Reachable on every row without opening the overview — collapsed, failed, archived.

  2. D2What does the new item carry?

    Your text as the title, unclassified; notes name the post and its link

    The classifier files it like any capture; the notes say where it came from.

  3. D4Does adding archive the post?

    No — the post stays put and the field stays open for another

    Departs from Comms’ Inbox item, which clears its row: a post isn’t an obligation.

  4. D5Keyboard?

    a opens the field on the selected row; Enter adds, Escape closes

    Every other verb has a key; a is free.

  5. D3How does an add confirm itself?

    A toast, “Added to Inbox”, linking to the Inbox

☞ open: your pick · → proposed: my call · ✓ settled: yours. Merging accepts every proposed row.

Not doing

No marker on the post saying it spawned items, no model-drafted suggestions, no due dates or folders from the field.

Working detail

Everything below is for the implementing agent. Approving needs only what’s above.

Context

The ticket: “Want some sort of toggleable free-form text field on a reader summary that lets me add something to the inbox.” Reading a post often prompts an action (try a tool, ask someone, follow up a claim); today the owner has to switch to Tasks and retype it, losing the link to what prompted it.

What exists:

  • frontend/components/reader/post-row.tsx — the row, its verb line (verbRowClass), its exit animation, and its row-scoped hotkeys via readerHotkeyAction in frontend/lib/reader/hotkeys.ts (i o e v j k, Escape). The same component serves the reading list and the archive (variant).
  • frontend/lib/reader/open-link.ts — postOpenLink(post): canonical URL, else Gmail permalink, else (research posts) the session URL, else nothing.
  • frontend/lib/stores/tasks-store.tsx — TasksProvider is mounted by the shell layout for every module, so useTaskActions().addTask is callable from the Reader. addTask already inserts optimistically, calls POST /api/items, reconciles, rolls back, toasts “Couldn’t add task” and rethrows on failure. Its AddTaskInput has no sourceUrl/notes yet; createItemSchema and makeOptimisticItem already accept both.
  • Precedent: Comms’ “Make an Inbox item” (/api/comms/messages/[id]/inbox-item) — one click, title derived from the message, the row clears. This story differs on purpose (D4): free-form text, and the post stays.

Epic ALF-232 (D1) says “nothing auto-converts a post into a task”. This story honours that — every item is typed by the owner — and adds no reader table, column or migration.

Decision records

D3How does an add confirm itself? · settled

B, the owner’s pick: once addTask resolves, the field calls showToast('Added to Inbox', 'default', '/'). The toast viewport renders a toast with an href as a ViewLink, so its text navigates to the Inbox and dismisses the toast — the same seam the story-gate toast uses. It fires on success only, never optimistically, so a failed add shows the store’s failure toast alone (D7). Several adds in a row stack one toast each; accepted.

Considered: A, a muted “✓ Added to Inbox: “<text>”” line under the field.

D1Where does the field live? · proposed

Considered: inside the overview panel (only reachable after expanding, and absent on rows with no panel — a pending or failed row); a popover (heavier, and a popover on a phone covers the post). The field renders directly under reader-row-verbs, outside the overview collapse, so it works on every state of the row — list and archive variants, pending, failed, refused, research. The verb sits after Overview and before Retry/Archive, a ghost button with lucide’s Inbox icon; open, it takes bg-secondary and aria-expanded="true", and pressing it again closes the field.

D2What does the new item carry? · proposed

Title and raw_capture are the trimmed text, exactly as the capture box sends them; item_type is unclassified so the existing classifier sweep files it. Considered: prefixing the title with the post’s title (noisy, and it would bias the classifier); creating it as a task (skips the classifier the owner relies on). source_url is postOpenLink(post).href. Task rows don’t render source_url today, so the notes carry the provenance visibly (D8). A post with no link sends no source_url and omits the notes’ URL line.

D4Does adding archive the post? · proposed

No. Comms clears its row because a message turned into a task is no longer an obligation in two places; a post is reading material and may prompt several items. Archiving stays its own verb. After an add the field clears and keeps focus; Escape or the verb closes it.

D5Keyboard · proposed

a maps to a new ReaderHotkeyAction 'capture': on the selected row it opens the field (or focuses it if already open). Once the input has focus, isHotkeyBlocked already stops every reader hotkey — including the list’s Escape-deselect — so typing j, e or a can’t navigate or archive. The input handles Enter (submit) and Escape (close the field, return focus to the verb) itself.

Implementation decisions

D6 · Write through addTask, not a new route

Extend AddTaskInput with optional sourceUrl and notes and pass them into createInput. The Inbox count and list update optimistically in the same store the Inbox reads, with no new endpoint, RPC or migration. A Reader-specific route (the Comms pattern) is only needed when a second table must change atomically, which isn’t the case here.

D7 · Failure restores the draft

The field clears as soon as you submit (optimistic). If addTask rejects, the store has already rolled back and toasted; the field puts the submitted text back if it’s still empty and the field is still open, and the D3 success toast is not shown.

D8 · Notes format

A pure helper, readerCaptureNotes(post, eyebrow, href) in frontend/lib/reader/capture.ts: line 1 From the Reader: “<post.title>” — <eyebrow> (the eyebrow from postEyebrow, so Instapaper and research rows read right); line 2 the href when there is one. Unit-tested, so the format is pinned in one place.

D9 · The field’s state is the row’s own

Open/closed and the draft are useState in a new ReaderInboxCapture component, not a coordination store: several rows may have it open at once, like the overview. Archiving or sending a row with a draft discards the draft along with the row.

Acceptance criteria

  • Every reader row (list and archive, any summary state, research included) shows an “Add to Inbox” verb between Overview and Archive.
  • The verb toggles a focused one-line field with an Add button beneath the verb line; the verb carries aria-expanded.
  • Adding creates one unclassified Inbox item whose title is the trimmed text, whose source_url is the row’s open link, and whose notes match D8.
  • A post with no open link creates the item without source_url and with a one-line note.
  • After an add the post stays in the list, the field is empty, still open and focused.
  • A successful add shows one toast, “Added to Inbox”, whose text navigates to the Inbox.
  • On the selected row, a opens and focuses the field and its keycap shows on the verb (md and up); Escape in the field closes it without deselecting the row.
  • The new item appears in the Inbox without a reload.

Review focus

  • Blank or whitespace-only text → Add disabled, Enter creates nothing.
  • Typing j, k, e, i, o, v or a in the field → only text, no hotkey fires.
  • A failed POST /api/items → the text is back in the field and only the failure toast shows.
  • Two rows with the field open → each keeps its own draft; adding from one doesn’t touch the other.

Out of scope, risks & unknowns

  • Marker on the post. Showing “2 items from this post” would need a reader_posts ↔ items link (a column or join table and a migration). Nothing asks for it yet; the notes and source_url keep the link on the item side.
  • Model-suggested items from the overview: contradicts epic D1’s “nothing auto-converts”, and would be its own story.
  • Capture-box extras (the <project>: prefix, folder, due date): the classifier and the Inbox already handle these after capture.
  • Risk: the new verb lengthens the verb line; at phone width it wraps to a second line on rows that already show Retry. Check the 375 px viewport in the Storybook snapshots.
  • Risk: existing PostRow / ReadingListView baselines move because of the new verb; regenerate them through the snapshot gate, never by hand.
